Colocation market reality: what changed in 2026

The buying environment moved in four ways. Here's what the questions used to be, and what they are now.

Space VS. Power

Before: renting square feet, with power sized to fit the footprint.

6–12 months

That is the planning window for a megawatt or more. Most of what is available in the near term sits below 1 MW, and a listed figure is not automatically one contiguous block you can use. A campus total can be split across rooms or phases, and a note saying «immediate» can sit beside an install date two quarters out. Ask whether the number is one room or several, and make sure every quarter comes with a year. This is a planning window, not a guaranteed installation date.

Rack Density

Before: 3 kW to 5 kW air-cooled cabinets, and cooling was rarely the question.

~35 kW per rack

That is the honest number to specify when you ask a provider for air-cooled capacity. An 8-GPU HGX or DGX B300 server draws roughly 14–16 kW. One per rack lands near 16–18 kW, and almost any legacy facility takes it. Two per rack reaches 32–36 kW, the realistic ceiling for pure air with hot-aisle containment. Three or four needs rear-door heat exchangers, which is liquid-assisted no matter what the data sheet calls it.

Deployment Size

Before: bring whatever you have, and providers would quote almost any footprint.

100–500 kW

This is the footprint many larger providers are actively seeking right now. Below it, you have options and shorter decisions. At 1 MW and up, investment-grade credit becomes the main gate, and some providers want to meet you before committing a full megawatt. Without IGC, a few will start you near 300 kW and consider expansion later. Expansion is not reserved capacity unless it is in writing.

Pricing & Terms

Before: published list rates you could compare side by side.

200 $–250 per kW/month

That is the October planning band for 100–500 kW, covering space and power. Under 100 kW runs 225 $–250. One megawatt and up runs 185 $–250, where the 185 $ end is build-to-suit rather than ready-now space. The bands overlap on purpose. Bigger does not reliably mean cheaper, and at size the real differences are availability, credit and delivery date. Terms in our current inventory run from 12 months to 36 and longer.
